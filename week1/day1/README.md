1. What is Salesforce?

Salesforce is a cloud-based Customer Relationship Management (CRM) platform used by companies to manage customers, sales, and business processes in one place.

It helps organizations improve customer relationships, automate workflows, and increase productivity.

2. What is CRM?

CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management.

It is a system that helps businesses:

Store customer information
Track interactions
Manage sales and leads
Improve customer relationships
3. Salesforce Developer Role

A Salesforce Developer is responsible for:

Building custom applications on Salesforce
Creating and managing objects and data models
Automating business processes
Writing logic using Apex and Lightning components (advanced stage)
4. Salesforce Objects & Data Model

I learned the basics of Salesforce objects:

Account → Company or organization
Contact → Individual person linked to account
Lead → Potential customer
Opportunity → Possible sales deal

These objects form the backbone of Salesforce data structure.
