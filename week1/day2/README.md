Day 2: Salesforce Data Objects & Real-World Mapping
1. Understanding Core Salesforce Objects

Today I learned the fundamental objects used in Salesforce to store and manage business data.

Account: Represents a company or organization. It defines who the customer is.
Contact: Represents an individual person linked to an account.
Opportunity: Represents a potential deal or revenue-generating sales process.

These objects are connected and form the base of Salesforce CRM structure.

2. Real-World Mapping Example

To understand the concept better, I mapped it to a college admission system:

Salesforce Object	Real-World Equivalent	Meaning
Account	School / Junior College	Institution where the student studied
Contact	Student	Individual applying for admission
Opportunity	Admission Application	Process of getting selected for a seat

This helped me understand how Salesforce represents real business scenarios using structured data.
